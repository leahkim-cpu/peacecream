# PeaceCream dessert photo prompts

Save each result as `assets/products/<type>.jpg` (square, 1200×1200), then set its path in
`TYPE_PHOTOS` in `pc-catalog.js` (e.g. `cake: 'assets/products/cake.jpg'`).
Optimize with: `sips -Z 1200 -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 82 IN.png --out assets/products/cake.jpg`

**Shared style (add to every prompt):** professional food photography, photorealistic, real dessert,
soft natural window light from the upper left, gentle soft shadows, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens,
pastel seamless backdrop, minimal elegant styling like a Japanese dessert boutique, dreamy and cute but
realistic textures, square 1:1, high resolution, no text, no logos, no hands, no cartoon, no 3D render.
Match the look of the PeaceCream gelato cup photo (warm pink backdrop, creamy highlights).

1. **cake** — A slice-ready round strawberry and vanilla shortcake on a small cream ceramic plate: soft golden sponge layers, fresh whipped vanilla cream, glossy halved strawberries on top and between layers, a few crumbs. Soft strawberry-milk pink backdrop.
2. **macaron** — Six lavender and vanilla macarons, some stacked, on a pale lavender backdrop: smooth matte shells with ruffled "feet", thick creamy vanilla filling, one macaron slightly turned to show the filling, a sprig of dried lavender.
3. **cookie** — Three thick chocolate chip cookies, golden-brown edges, soft centers, melted chocolate pieces, one broken in half showing a gooey inside, on a vanilla-cream backdrop with a few loose chocolate chunks.
4. **donut** — Two soft honey-glazed ring donuts with a glossy, slightly dripping amber glaze, fluffy texture visible where one is torn, a small wooden honey dipper, warm peach backdrop.
5. **mochi** — Three round daifuku mochi on a small ceramic dish: two pale matcha-green, one dusted white, one cut open showing smooth red bean paste, soft powdery potato-starch dusting, mint-green backdrop.
6. **cupcake** — Two vanilla cupcakes in pastel paper liners with fluffy swirled raspberry-pink buttercream, a fresh raspberry and tiny pearl sprinkles on top, one with a bite showing the light vanilla crumb, baby-pink backdrop.
